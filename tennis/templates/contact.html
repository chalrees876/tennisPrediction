Frequently Asked Questions

How often are predictions updated?

Predictions are updated daily as new match data becomes available. Upcoming matches are typically available 24-48 hours before match time.

What's the accuracy of your predictions?

Our ensemble model maintains approximately 65% accuracy on historical data. However, past performance doesn't guarantee future results, and tennis inherently involves uncertainty.

Can I use this data for betting?

This platform is for informational and educational purposes only. We provide data-driven insights, but users should exercise their own judgment and comply with local laws regarding sports betting.

How can I contribute or report data issues?

We welcome feedback on data accuracy and feature suggestions. Please use the contact form above to report any issues or share ideas for improvement.
